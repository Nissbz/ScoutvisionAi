How ScoutVision AI Works

A five-stage intelligence framework transforming raw data into structured transfer intelligence.

1
Multi-Source Data Integration

Designed framework for aggregating match performance metrics, injury history, tactical context, physical data, and league strength coefficients from structured public and federated sources.

2
Performance Normalization

Developed modeling logic to standardize player performance across leagues — adjusting for league quality, team strength, tactical role, and playing time via per-90 metrics.

3
Feature Engineering & Player Profiling

Constructed advanced indicators including efficiency metrics, defensive contribution index, adaptability score, injury risk probability, and pressure-performance consistency index.

4
Cross-League Simulation

Constructed probabilistic simulation approach to project performance in target league and team environments — estimating expected output ranges and performance variance.

5
Decision-Ready Output

Structured decision output system delivering projected performance ranges, risk assessments, adaptability scores, and confidence levels — designed to enhance scout judgment, not replace it.
