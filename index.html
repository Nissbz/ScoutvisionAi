Independent Concept Project

ScoutVision AI

A structured decision intelligence engine for cross-league player projection and transfer risk modeling.

Designed to support smarter recruitment decisions through normalization logic, probabilistic simulation, and embedded risk evaluation.

The Problem

Football recruitment remains fragmented across leagues, contexts, and performance environments. Clubs evaluate players using inconsistent benchmarks, leading to distorted comparisons, inefficient scouting allocation, and transfer decisions exposed to significant uncertainty.

Strategic Impact

Improved Capital Allocation

Structures recruitment as a probabilistic investment decision rather than a subjective comparison process.

Cross-League Projection

Simulates performance translation between leagues using contextual normalization logic.

Embedded Risk Modeling

Integrates availability history, performance variance, and adaptability indicators into evaluation output.

Intelligence Pipeline

1. Data Integration Layer

Aggregates match performance metrics, availability history, tactical role context, and league strength indicators.

2. Performance Normalization

Adjusts output based on league quality, team structure, and competitive environment.

3. Feature Engineering

Builds structured player profiles across efficiency, adaptability, and consistency dimensions.

4. Probabilistic Simulation

Generates performance ranges in target leagues instead of deterministic single-point projections.

5. Decision Output Framework

Produces structured projection insights and contextual risk indicators for recruitment teams.

System Architecture Overview

ScoutVision AI is conceptualized as a modular decision intelligence system built on structured data ingestion, contextual normalization logic, probabilistic modeling, and transparent output architecture. The framework prioritizes interpretability and integration into existing scouting workflows.

Design Principles

Transparency over Black-Box Prediction

Outputs remain interpretable and structured rather than opaque algorithmic scores.

Probabilistic Thinking

Performance expressed as ranges with embedded uncertainty, not fixed projections.

Risk Embedded by Default

Every evaluation integrates availability trends and contextual performance variance.

Human Judgment Remains Central

The engine enhances — not replaces — scouting expertise.

ScoutVision AI is an independent conceptual project exploring structured decision intelligence systems in competitive football recruitment environments.
