AI-Powered Player Projection Engine

ScoutVision AI

A predictive framework designed to surface undervalued players and structure smarter transfer decisions.

Football recruitment is expensive, inefficient, and uncertain — clubs consistently overpay while hidden gems go unnoticed across leagues.

The Problem

  • Scouting operations are expensive and yield inconsistent results.
  • Transfer mistakes cost clubs millions — overpaying for talent that doesn't adapt.
  • Undervalued players across smaller leagues go completely unnoticed.

The Solution

  • Surface undervalued talent before the market catches up.
  • Reduce scouting inefficiencies through automated pre-filtering.
  • Cross-league normalization enables fair, context-aware comparison.

Efficiency

Target meaningful reductions in scouting inefficiencies.

Allocation

Improve capital allocation efficiency in transfer decisions.

Discovery

Framework designed to surface undervalued players across leagues.

How ScoutVision AI Works

1
Multi-Source Data Integration

Aggregating match metrics, injury history, and league strength coefficients.

2
Performance Normalization

Standardizing performance across leagues, adjusting for team strength and tactical role.

3
Feature Engineering & Profiling

Advanced indicators: adaptability scores, injury risk, and pressure-consistency index.
