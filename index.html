Independent Concept Project

ScoutVision AI

A structured decision intelligence engine for cross-league player projection and transfer risk modeling.

Designed to support smarter recruitment decisions through normalization logic, probabilistic simulation, and embedded risk evaluation.

The Problem

Football recruitment remains fragmented across leagues and performance environments. Clubs evaluate players using inconsistent benchmarks, leading to distorted comparisons, inefficient scouting allocation, and transfer decisions exposed to structural uncertainty.

Strategic Impact

Improved Capital Allocation

Transforms recruitment evaluation into a structured probabilistic investment framework.

Cross-League Simulation

Projects performance translation between competitive environments with contextual normalization logic.

Embedded Risk Intelligence

Integrates availability trends, performance variance, and adaptability indicators.

Intelligence Pipeline

Data Integration Layer

Aggregates match metrics, contextual team factors, and league strength coefficients.

Normalization Engine

Adjusts player output across environments to enable structured comparison.

Feature Engineering

Builds multidimensional player profiles across efficiency and consistency axes.

Probabilistic Simulation

Generates performance ranges rather than deterministic projections.

Decision Output

Produces structured risk-adjusted recruitment insights.

Design Principles

Transparency

Structured modeling over black-box prediction.

Probabilistic Thinking

Performance expressed as confidence ranges.

Risk Embedded by Default

Uncertainty integrated into every projection.

Human Judgment Central

Supports — not replaces — scouting expertise.

ScoutVision AI is an independent conceptual project exploring structured decision intelligence systems in competitive football recruitment environments.
